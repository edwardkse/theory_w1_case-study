GROUP ACTIVITY: COMMUNICATION IN ACTION

Understanding Malaysia's Movement Control Order (MCO), 2020

Background

On 16 March 2020, the Malaysian Prime Minister announced the implementation of the Movement Control Order (MCO) to control the spread of COVID-19.


The MCO took effect on 18 March 2020. The announcement was communicated through television, news media and other communication channels.


The announcement affected the daily lives of Malaysians, including students, families, businesses and workers. People had to understand the new restrictions, follow government instructions and adjust their everyday activities.
